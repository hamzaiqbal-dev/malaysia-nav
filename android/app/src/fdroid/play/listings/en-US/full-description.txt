Sampai is a navigation app made in Malaysia.

• Offline maps: download a region once and use it without mobile data
• Turn-by-turn navigation with voice guidance
• Search for places, addresses and categories
• Bookmarks and track recording, stored on your phone
• No ads and no trackers

Map data comes from OpenStreetMap contributors and may be incomplete or out of date. Always obey traffic laws and road signs, and do not use the phone while driving.
