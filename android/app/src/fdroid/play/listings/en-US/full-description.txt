Sampai is a navigation app made in Malaysia for Malaysians: Bahasa Melayu first, built to be reliable, and designed around the things drivers here actually need.

<b>TOLLS IN RM:</b> See the toll total of your route while you drive. Toll plazas on LDP, SPRINT, KESAS, DUKE, MEX, SMART, NPE, Besraya, SILK, Guthrie, SUKE, AKLEH, LATAR, KLK and more are priced per crossing; PLUS and LPT fares are being added.

<b>BAHASA MELAYU FIRST:</b> Menu, search and voice guidance in Bahasa Melayu, with English, 中文 and தமிழ் one tap away.

<b>WORKS OFFLINE:</b> Maps of Malaysia, Singapore and the region are stored on your phone. Search, routing and turn-by-turn navigation keep working in Sabah, Sarawak, on kampung roads and anywhere the signal drops. No data plan needed.

<b>CLEAR WHEN DRIVING:</b> A large turn arrow, lane guidance, your speed next to the speed limit, and a night mode that is easy on the eyes.

<b>MALAYSIAN PLACES:</b> Surau and masjid, R&R stops, petrol stations, mamak and kopitiam, klinik kesihatan, JPJ, PDRM, Puspakom, EV chargers and more, searchable in Bahasa Melayu.

<b>PRIVATE:</b> No account, no tracking, no ads. Your location and your trips stay on your phone.

Sampai is built on the open-source Organic Maps engine and OpenStreetMap data, improved every day by volunteers across Malaysia. Found a missing road or a wrong name? Fix it in the app and help every other Malaysian driver.

Sampai is in early development: the Bahasa Melayu interface and voice are being completed, and live road reports (jams, accidents, police, floods) are on the way.
