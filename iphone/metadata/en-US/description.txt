Sampai is a navigation app made in Malaysia for Malaysians: Bahasa Melayu first, built to be reliable, and designed around the things drivers here actually need.

BAHASA MELAYU FIRST: Menu, search and voice guidance in Bahasa Melayu, with English and 中文 one tap away.

WORKS OFFLINE: Maps of Malaysia, Singapore and the region are stored on your phone. Search, routing and turn-by-turn navigation keep working in Sabah, Sarawak, on kampung roads and anywhere the signal drops. No data plan needed.

CLEAR WHEN DRIVING: A large turn arrow, lane guidance, your speed next to the speed limit, and a night mode that is easy on the eyes.

MALAYSIAN PLACES: Surau and masjid, R&R stops, petrol stations, mamak and kopitiam, klinik kesihatan, JPJ, PDRM, Puspakom, EV chargers and more, searchable in Bahasa Melayu.

PRIVATE: No account, no tracking, no ads. Your location and your trips stay on your phone.

Sampai uses OpenStreetMap data, improved every day by volunteers across Malaysia. Found a missing road or a wrong name? Fix it in the app and help every other Malaysian driver.

Sampai is in early development: the Bahasa Melayu interface and voice are being completed, and live road reports (jams, accidents, police, floods) are on the way.
